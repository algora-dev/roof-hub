## Mega-answer page pattern

1. Direct answer in plain NZ terminology.
2. Conditions that materially change the decision.
3. Multiple legitimate pathways, including the least-invasive one.
4. What each pathway can and cannot solve.
5. Practitioner examples, explicitly labelled as commercial/project evidence.
6. Manufacturer constraints where the question is product/system-specific.
7. Government/industry/safety rules where applicable.
8. RoofHub synthesis.
9. Questions to ask a contractor / what an inspection needs to establish.
10. Contextual tool or Assistant action.

The content must not reduce a complex condition to “X years old = reroof” or “repair quote below Y% = repair”.
