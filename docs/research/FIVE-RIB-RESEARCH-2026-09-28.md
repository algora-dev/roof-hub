# Five-rib research register, 28 September 2026

## Purpose and publication scope

This is the evidence register for the updated `/roofing/five-rib` reference guide and targeted changes to `/guides/corrugated-vs-five-rib`. It is not an installed-rate survey or product approval.

The five-rib guide cites **39 unique source pages**, with **8 profile entries from 6 manufacturers**, **5 priced listings from 4 businesses**, **3 held/excluded listings**, and **7 project accounts from 6 publishers**. There are 28 new source records and 6 new project records in this release; the other citations reuse existing records.

Review dates indicate when evidence was examined, not when a supplier last set its price or guaranteed stock. Merchant evidence retrieved through indexed page text can be older than the review date. Prices must be reconfirmed before purchase.

## What was and was not collected

- Manufacturer web specifications and manufacturer-authored technical-library entries.
- Direct supplier prices with their original units, plus supplier terms where they establish GST.
- First-party project narratives, including businesses publishing through EBOSS or ArchiPro.
- NZMRM technical guidance for terminology and conditional installation statements.
- No copied contractor photographs or full third-party articles.
- No independent invoice audit, site inspection, product testing or structural design.
- No review scores, guaranteed cheapest-product hierarchy, national installed range or automatic estimator rate changes.
- PDF technical drawings that could not be retrieved were not treated as read. The Trimrib drawing-directory entries establish that different details exist, not their full installation dimensions.

### Exclusions and disputes

- Roof Crowd Plumbdek: dimensions recovered, no unambiguous selected public price recovered.
- R&C MAXAM five-rib: title and body disagree about profile. Do not reuse its corrugated description as a five-rib price.
- DIY Supply Trade Me: closed listing, not a current offer.
- Hi Five: 765 mm overview versus 755 mm performance-table cover. Both remain visible.
- Trimrib: different swage/branch variants; project-specific 760 mm examples do not establish universal ordering dimensions. The retrieved library did not substantiate a complete BMT choice table, so the manufacturer comparison asks the branch to confirm it.
- Some large retailer pages were usable only through indexed primary text. The source notes retain that limitation; the status does not mean a live checkout was completed.

## Technical correction: flashing labour

The page no longer treats hand-notching as mandatory for every five-rib roof. NZMRM sections 8.8.1 and 8.8.2 describe soft-edge options for low trapezoidal profiles and a preference for notched treatment above 30 mm rib height. Roofing Industries' Trimrib directory lists both soft-edge and scribed/notched ridge details.

The practical conclusion is to price the selected detail and roof geometry. This does not disprove a roofer's experience of greater labour on particular five-rib roofs. It prevents a contributor's estimating allowance becoming an unsupported universal technical rule. **No existing labour allowance is changed by this release.**

## Worked examples and calculators

- Five-rib size examples use the unrounded Bitz conditional conversion for 100, 150, 200 and 250 m² of actual sloping roof area. No labour or ancillary work is included.
- Shared sheet-budget primitive converts a single-pitch horizontal projection once. Effective cover includes side laps. Extra allowance and minimum order are explicit.
- A fixed 3.6 m sheet listing is not used as a continuous-length purchase preset.
- The new two-quote worksheet begins with visibly labelled hypothetical $25/lm inclusive-GST examples at 760 and 840 mm. It calculates arithmetic, not a product ranking.
- Unknown GST blocks the comparison. Either quote's minimum order can apply independently. Blank, negative, nonfinite and overflowing inputs are rejected.
- Reader inputs are not silently transferred to the detailed estimator or used to overwrite its rates.

## Project selection

Seven accounts illustrate scope, rather than supply a fabricated installed-price dataset:

1. Point Wells, Metalhartt: Trimrib alongside membrane, rainwater and skylight work.
2. Hibiscus Coast, HDK: an existing published Trimrib reroof account reused from the research library.
3. Splash Hut, Steel & Tube/JD Homes via EBOSS: a dated corrugated-to-Plumbdek replacement story.
4. Eastern Beach, Mastercraft: metal-tile replacement within a broader renovation.
5. Garnet Road, Metalhartt: different roofing and wall-cladding systems in one development.
6. No-swage Trimrib, Aspect Roofing via ArchiPro: detailed junctions; broad project budget not used as a roof contract price.
7. Canterbury rural building, Wide Span Sheds: published building dimensions are not a measured roof surface.

Every project retains `price: null`, `areaM2: null`, and `imagePermission: not-obtained`. Syndicated accounts and multiple units do not multiply the evidence count. The original publisher retains its imagery and receives a source link.
