# Pressed Metal Tile Reference Guide: research register

Review date: 29 September 2026. Release: v0.9.

## Scope and evidence standard

The page is a modern new-roof selection and pricing guide first. Replacement is secondary. It is not a national market-share survey or a recommendation to hide damaged framing beneath another covering.

There are 37 cited source pages, not 37 independent confirmations of the same claim. Nine selected product profiles come from Gerard's current NZ family. Four displayed guide-price observations come from three publishers. Six project accounts come from four publishers. Three newly collected examples are explicitly new-home/showhome accounts; the Kumeu account is an appearance gallery with no verified construction date. Two existing replacement accounts provide separate scope lessons.

## Key findings and decisions

1. **Gerard / Metrotile:** Gerard's NZ site says Metrotile is now Gerard. Keep legacy product names on historic projects; do not count those brands as two independent current manufacturers.
2. **Material prices:** No new first-quality NZ per-panel retail quotation was verified with product, unit, GST and coverage together. General contractor material categories, overseas retail, used/seconds listings and installed guide prices do not fill that gap. No market panel rate is auto-filled or inferred by subtracting labour.
3. **Published installed prices:** Arcline's 10 February 2024 satin $60/m² and textured $65/m² guides include material and installation. GST and tile area basis are not established. Roofing Systems currently displays $90/m² including installation and GST, but the article has no publication date or complete scope. Delta's 2025 $150 to $210/m² stone-chip/Metrotile replacement guide includes GST, scaffold, removal and disposal. The observations are not pooled.
4. **Immutable observations:** Existing `tt-01` remains provisional. The renewed Roofing Systems page observation is a separate record, `tile-rs-20260929`, with `rangeEligible: false`. All older observation objects remain unchanged.
5. **Supply model:** NZMRM describes manufacturer-appointed, trained installers and tiles not normally supplied to other installers. That supports explaining limited retail visibility, not making claims about supplier margins or guaranteeing no public retail price exists elsewhere.
6. **Support assemblies:** Conventional tile and CF appraisal summaries differ. The CF summary identifies batten alternatives for CF Shake, CF Shingle and CF Slate. Do not extrapolate that alternative to Calibre. The quantity tool supplies no batten gauge or support design.
7. **Conflicting figures:** CF Slate lists 1250 by 255 mm cover and 3.20 panels/m²; those dimensions imply approximately 3.14. Calibre lists 1250 by 250 mm, 0.32 m² cover, and a 3.12 density field carrying a unit typo. Their automatic presets are disabled. These may be publication or set-out issues; request confirmation rather than declaring a design defect.
8. **New-build examples:** Fowler's Huapai home and Abron's Brookfield showhome identify tile roofs. Published house floor areas are not roof areas. Gerard's Kerikeri CF Shake gallery supplies an architectural new-build example. The Kumeu Shake gallery shows application and colour, not a dated construction record.
9. **Replacement correction:** The Mastercraft Eastern Beach page actually says T-rib, not Trimrib. Its existing source/project IDs are retained for compatibility; the visible description and review date are corrected. The Tamahere account replaced asphalt, not old pressed metal. It illustrates substrate repairs, not tile failure frequency.
10. **Maintenance and warranty:** Use the current product instructions and environmental terms. Do not equate a warranty duration with observed life. The roof maintenance text discourages water blasting for organic growth; separate wall-cladding pressure instructions are not roof-cleaning directions. No pressure limits are republished as DIY instructions.

## Pricing gaps that remain

The important missing evidence is a written current supplier/installer panel or system quotation with exact profile, finish, effective quantity, tax, accessories, support supply, installation responsibility, delivery and validity. A private quote can later be recorded as private input with permission to publish any aggregate or attributed details. The user's working $8 to $10.50/m² labour range remains an internal input and is not represented as independent market evidence or changed in this release.

## Calculator methodology

- Use roof surface area, or convert horizontal roof projection once using area / cos(pitch).
- Multiply actual area by the selected published planning panel density.
- Add only the user's extra percentage, then round whole panels upward.
- Apply minimum order first, then whole-pack rounding. Defaults: 0% extra, no minimum, one panel per unit.
- Seven profile densities may populate automatically. CF Slate, Calibre and custom products require a supplier-confirmed density.
- Price only when the reader supplies a per-panel quote. Unknown GST remains unknown. Excluding-GST inputs use 15%; including-GST inputs are not taxed again.
- Optional batten quantity is actual area / user-provided gauge in metres. It is not a structural design, cutting list or complete purchase schedule. Calibre does not offer that option.
- No panel price, batten rate, installer labour or estimator rate is supplied by this new worksheet. No automatic transfer of measurements is claimed.

## Source access and limitations

HTML product schedules, contractor/builder pages and the NZMRM code were used directly. The CF appraisal is supported by its indexed Building CodeHub description; the underlying CF PDF was not retrievable in this environment. The conventional appraisal and 2026 warranty/care PDF text were available. PDF screenshot attempts on the appraisal/care documents failed, so no unread graphical table was used to set quantitative design or warranty limits. Do not claim full document certification or independent project inspection.

No third-party project photography was downloaded or republished. Project cards link to the publisher's own account and images. No full competitor article or installation manual is included.
